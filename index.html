01 · Jio Industry Project · Research

ThermalView: Forecast-Aware Server Workload Placement

Temperature lags load by several minutes, so a server that looks cool when a job lands may already be heading towards its limit. ThermalView forecasts CPU temperature 5–30 minutes ahead and ranks servers by how hot they will be after the new job starts.

  • Evaluated on real telemetry from 40 nodes of the CINECA Marconi100 supercomputer.
  • Random Forest beats persistence by 8.7% (95% CI 7.1–10.2%), 12.4% on unseen nodes and 11.3% on an unseen month.
  • Forecast-aware placement cut time above the thermal limit by 14% vs coolest-server-first, in a simulator calibrated on real data.
  • Leakage-safe evaluation: chronological splits, held-out nodes, block-bootstrap CIs, conformal prediction intervals.
  • Live operator console, REST API, Prometheus and Grafana, Docker, CI on GitHub Actions.
